1. Copyright positioning

This report identifies software expression and implementation integration visible in the repository. It is not a legal opinion, ownership determination, novelty opinion, or guarantee of registration. Copyright scope and applicant/authorship details should be confirmed with the University and qualified counsel.

The Indian Copyright Office states that computer software/programmes can be registered as software and calls for source and object code. Current online instructions describe a PDF of at least the first 10 and last 10 source pages, or all source if under 20 pages, with no blocked or redacted portions. Confirm current filing requirements before submission.

3. Limits of originality claims

The general idea of resume-to-job matching, weighted arithmetic, text normalization, model fine-tuning, and forecasting is not claimed as exclusive. Third-party libraries, pretrained Qwen model weights, taxonomies, source-platform data, and external datasets are not claimed as owned by ResumeIntel. The repository currently has no selected software license; dependency notices and dataset rights require separate review.

The claimed contribution is the authored source-code expression and its particular integration, user interface, data schemas, rule configuration, and workflows as evidenced in this repository. Do not assert “first”, “unique in the market”, or validated superiority without comparative research.
